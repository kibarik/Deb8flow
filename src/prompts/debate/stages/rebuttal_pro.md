## Your Mission: DESTROY THEIR ARGUMENTS

The CON has raised concerns. Your job is to:
1. **Directly address EVERY major point** they made
2. **Show why their concerns are overstated, mistaken, or already addressed**
3. **Reveal weaknesses in their critique**
4. **Strengthen your original position with new evidence**

## Structure Your Rebuttal (BE COMPREHENSIVE)

### 1. Acknowledge and Reframe (1 paragraph)
- Acknowledge their strongest point(s)
- Reframe them as opportunities rather than showstoppers
- Show you understand their perspective

### 2. Systematic Point-by-Point Refutation (3-5 paragraphs)
For EACH major concern the CON raised:
- State their argument clearly
- Provide direct counter-evidence from the PRD
- Explain why their concern is misplaced
- Show how the risk is mitigated or manageable

### 3. Expose Weaknesses in Their Critique (2-3 paragraphs)
- What did they misunderstand about the PRD?
- What risks did they exaggerate?
- What did they fail to consider?
- Show the flaws in their reasoning

### 4. Strengthen Your Core Case (2-3 paragraphs)
- Return to your strongest arguments
- Add new evidence or perspectives
- Show why your position is even stronger than initially presented

### 5. Closing Statement (1 paragraph)
- Summarize why their objections don't outweigh the opportunity
- Reiterate why this project should proceed

## Quality Guidelines

- **BE THOROUGH**: Address every significant concern - leaving points unanswered looks weak
- **BE SPECIFIC**: Reference exact PRD content that counters their claims
- **BE RESPECTFUL BUT FIRM**: Don't be defensive - be confident and authoritative
- **BE STRUCTURED**: Clear organization makes your rebuttal more persuasive
- **USE ALL AVAILABLE SPACE**: Short responses suggest you don't have good answers

## Effective Rebuttal Techniques

1. **Direct Refutation**: "Their concern about X is addressed in section Y where we state..."
2. **Contextualization**: "While they mention risk X, the full context shows..."
3. **Evidence-Based**: "The PRD provides specific data showing..."
4. **Prioritization**: "Even if their concern is valid, it doesn't outweigh the benefits because..."
5. **Reframing**: "What they see as a weakness is actually a strength because..."
