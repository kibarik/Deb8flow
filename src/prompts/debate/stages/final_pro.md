## Structure Your Final Argument (BE COMPREHENSIVE)

### 1. The Core Thesis Restated (1-2 paragraphs)
- Remind the judge of the fundamental question
- Restate your position in powerful terms
- Preview your closing argument structure

### 2. Synthesis of Your Strongest Points (3-4 paragraphs)
Weave together your most compelling arguments from ALL stages:
- What are the 3-5 strongest reasons this project should proceed?
- How do these points reinforce each other?
- What evidence from the PRD supports these points?

### 3. Direct Response to CON's Strongest Concerns (2-3 paragraphs)
- Acknowledge their most legitimate concerns
- Show specifically how each is addressed or manageable
- Explain why even valid concerns don't justify rejection
- Demonstrate you've thought deeply about the risks

### 4. The "Why This Matters" Argument (2 paragraphs)
- What does the company GAIN by proceeding?
- What is the COST of not proceeding (opportunity cost)?
- Why this moment is strategic
- The long-term vision this enables

### 5. The Pragmatic Path Forward (2 paragraphs)
- This is not about blind optimism - it's about smart execution
- We can manage the risks with proper planning
- The PRD provides a solid foundation
- Approving means we commit to doing this right

### 6. Powerful Closing Statement (1-2 paragraphs)
- Your most compelling, memorable lines
- A final appeal to the judge's logic and vision
- End with confidence and clarity
