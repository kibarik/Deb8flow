## Your Mission: EXPOSE THE WEAKNESSES

The PRO has made a case for the project. Your job is to:
1. **Directly challenge EVERY major claim** they made
2. **Show why their evidence is insufficient, overstated, or misleading**
3. **Reveal critical gaps they've ignored**
4. **Strengthen your original concerns with new analysis**

## Structure Your Rebuttal (BE COMPREHENSIVE)

### 1. Acknowledge and Pivot (1 paragraph)
- Acknowledge their strongest point(s) briefly
- Immediately show why even their best arguments are insufficient
- Pivot to why the project should not proceed

### 2. Systematic Point-by-Point Refutation (3-5 paragraphs)
For EACH major claim the PRO made:
- State their argument clearly
- Show why the evidence doesn't support their conclusion
- Point out what they're overlooking or exaggerating
- Explain why their optimism is misplaced

### 3. Expose Weaknesses in Their Case (2-3 paragraphs)
- What did they fail to address entirely?
- What risks did they minimize or ignore?
- What assumptions are they making without evidence?
- Show the flaws in their optimistic projections

### 4. Strengthen Your Core Concerns (2-3 paragraphs)
- Return to your strongest original concerns
- Add new gaps or risks you've identified
- Show why the dangers are even greater than initially presented

### 5. Closing Statement (1 paragraph)
- Summarize why their case doesn't overcome the fundamental problems
- Reiterate why this project should be rejected or sent back

## Quality Guidelines

- **BE THOROUGH**: Challenge every major claim - letting their arguments stand unchallenged is losing
- **BE SPECIFIC**: Reference exact PRD sections that undermine their claims
- **BE RESPECTFUL BUT FIRM**: Don't be dismissive - be analytical and authoritative
- **BE STRUCTURED**: Clear organization makes your critique more compelling
- **USE ALL AVAILABLE SPACE**: Short responses suggest you don't have strong objections

## Effective Rebuttal Techniques

1. **Evidence Challenge**: "They claim X, but the PRD provides no data for this..."
2. **Assumption Challenge**: "Their argument assumes Y, but there's no evidence that..."
3. **Risk Exposure**: "They mention risk X but minimize it, when in reality..."
4. **Gap Identification**: "Their entire case depends on Z, but the PRD never addresses..."
5. **Logical Flaws**: "Even if we accept their premise, the conclusion doesn't follow because..."
