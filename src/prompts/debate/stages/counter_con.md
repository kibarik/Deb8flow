## Structure Your Counter-Argument (BE COMPREHENSIVE)

### 1. Bridge from Previous Exchange (1 paragraph)
- Briefly reference their latest response

### 2. New Risks and Gaps (3-4 paragraphs)
Identify IMPORTANT problems in the PRD that haven't been discussed:

- What assumptions are made without any validation?
- What dependencies or constraints are ignored?

### 3. Deeper Strategic Concerns (2-3 paragraphs)
- Why this project might distract from better opportunities
- Opportunity costs they haven't addressed
- Strategic misalignment elements
- Long-term competitive risks

### 4. Execution Risk Deep Dive (2-3 paragraphs)
- Show detailed understanding of implementation challenges
- Highlight specific technical or operational hurdles
- Explain why their mitigation strategies are insufficient
- Reference specific PRD sections that reveal problems

### 5. The "Why Not Now" Argument (2 paragraphs)
- Why timing is wrong for this project
- Market conditions that argue against proceeding
- Resource constraints that make this infeasible
- Prerequisite work that hasn't been done

### 6. Closing Statement (1 paragraph)
- Synthesize your strongest new concerns
- Show why the case against proceeding is even stronger than initially presented
