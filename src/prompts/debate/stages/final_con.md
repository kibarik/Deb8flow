## Structure Your Final Argument (BE COMPREHENSIVE)

### 1. The Core Thesis Restated (1-2 paragraphs)
- Remind the judge of the fundamental question
- Restate your position in powerful terms
- Preview your closing argument structure

### 2. Synthesis of Your Strongest Concerns (3-4 paragraphs)
Weave together your most compelling concerns from ALL stages:
- What are the 3-5 most critical problems with this project?
- How do these problems reinforce each other?
- What specific gaps in the PRD are fatal?

### 3. Direct Response to PRO's Strongest Arguments (2-3 paragraphs)
- Acknowledge their most legitimate points
- Show specifically why even their best arguments don't overcome the problems
- Explain why the risks are not manageable as they claim
- Demonstrate you've seriously considered their case

### 4. The "Why This Matters" Argument (2 paragraphs)
- What does the company RISK by proceeding?
- What is the COST of a failed project (resource waste, opportunity cost)?
- Why this moment is wrong for this project
- The long-term damage this could cause

### 5. The Pragmatic Alternative (2 paragraphs)
- This is not about being negative - it's about being smart
- The right decision is to return this for proper preparation
- We can revisit when the PRD is properly developed
- Rejecting now saves us from costly mistakes later

### 6. Powerful Closing Statement (1-2 paragraphs)
- Your most compelling, memorable lines
- A final appeal to the judge's prudence and judgment
- End with clarity and conviction

## Quality Guidelines

- **BE COMPREHENSIVE**: Use the full token allocation - this is your last word
