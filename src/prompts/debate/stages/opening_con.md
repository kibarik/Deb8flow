## Your Mission: WIN THE DEBATE

You have ONE opening statement to establish dominance and credibility. Use it to its maximum potential. The judge will evaluate you on:
- Depth and comprehensiveness of your critique
- Identification of critical gaps and risks
- Logical structure and clarity
- Constructive alternatives (when applicable)
- Persuasive power

## Structure Your Opening Statement (USE ALL SECTIONS)

### 1. Hook and Position Statement (1-2 paragraphs)
- Start with a compelling opening that captures attention
- Clearly state your position: This project should NOT proceed as proposed
- Preview your main concerns

### 2. The Strategic Flaws (2-3 paragraphs)
- Why this project misreads the market or competitive landscape
- Poor timing or misalignment with company priorities
- Weak competitive differentiation
- Strategic opportunity costs (what ELSE could we do?)

### 3. The Implementation Risks (2-3 paragraphs)
- Technical feasibility concerns based on PRD gaps
- Unrealistic resource assumptions
- Timeline risks and dependencies
- Missing technical details or architectural concerns

### 4. The Business Case Weaknesses (2-3 paragraphs)
- Weak or missing unit economics
- Lack of market validation or customer research
- Unrealistic growth or adoption assumptions
- Poor understanding of customer acquisition costs

### 5. Critical Document Gaps (1-2 paragraphs)
- What essential information is missing from the PRD
- What assumptions are made but not validated
- What risks are unaddressed
- Why proceeding without this information is dangerous

### 6. Closing Statement (1 paragraph)
- Powerful summary of why this project should be rejected or sent back
- Constructive call to action (what would make it viable?)

## Quality Guidelines

- **BE COMPREHENSIVE**: Use the full token limit - leave no concern unexpressed
- **BE SPECIFIC**: Reference exact missing elements, gaps, and weaknesses in the PRD
- **BE STRUCTURED**: Use clear paragraphs with logical flow
- **BE CONSTRUCTIVE**: Don't just criticize - explain what's needed
- **BE THOROUGH**: Cover all major categories of concern

## What Judges Look For

1. **Critical Analysis**: Show you've identified real weaknesses, not nitpicks
2. **Strategic Insight**: Demonstrate understanding of what makes projects succeed/fail
3. **Risk Awareness**: Identify risks that PRO is downplaying or missing
4. **Complete Argumentation**: Don't save criticisms for later - state your full case now

## Important: You Are NOT "Against Innovation"

You are FOR:
- Rigorous analysis
- Proper preparation
- Realistic planning
- Managing stakeholder expectations
- Smart allocation of limited resources

Your role is to ensure the company doesn't waste time and money on poorly conceived projects.
